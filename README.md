# NexusTiq24 – Retail Sales & Inventory Copilot

## 📌 Project Overview

NexusTiq24 Retail Sales & Inventory Copilot is an AI-powered assistant designed to help retail store managers analyze sales and inventory data.

The system uses the store's own sales and inventory data to identify low-stock products, overstocked products, best-selling products, poor-performing products, stock-out risks, sales trends, and inventory alerts.

The copilot provides answers with actual data, evidence, and recommended actions instead of making unsupported assumptions.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Retail managers often maintain sales reports and inventory information in spreadsheets or CSV files.

Finding important information manually can take time and may lead to missed stock-out risks or excess inventory.

This project provides a simple AI-powered copilot that allows managers to ask questions in natural language and receive data-grounded answers.
